# About

**Kyber Physics** is a physics book built from first principles — foundations for the cybernetics stack.

## Who This Is For

Engineers, researchers and hobbies who want to understand Physics at the level where theory, mathematics, and intuition converge. If you've ever wondered *how* principles and laws link to each others, this book is for you. Also, this book is intentionally too be mathematical, but in a way that is not aim to be rigour but intuitive.

## The Thesis

Everything ties to one idea: **physical laws are constraints on dynamical systems — and understanding those constraints is the first step toward controlling them.**

Newtonian mechanics establishes the basic vocabulary of force, motion, and conservation. Analytical
mechanics reframes it all as optimization — the universe as a variational problem. Electromagnetism
reveals the field as the fundamental object. Circuits and electronics make the abstract concrete.
Optics shows how waves carry information. Signals and systems provide the language of filtering and
feedback — the bridge to control. Quantum mechanics shows what happens when the fundamental
constraint is uncertainty itself.

This book traces that arc, chapter by chapter, always with an eye toward the cybernetics framework
that unifies the Kyber series — because to control a system, you must first understand the laws
that govern it.
